What is ArtMoots?

ArtMoots was created to feel more personal than traditional social media. Instead of chasing trends or algorithms, the platform focuses on creativity, expression, inspiration, and community.

Whether you're a beginner artist posting sketches for fun or an experienced creator building a portfolio, ArtMoots is meant to feel welcoming, calm, and community-driven.
